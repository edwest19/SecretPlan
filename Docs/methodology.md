# SecretPlan Methodology

## Overview

SecretPlan is an open-source prompt framework intended to help Medicare beneficiaries compare Medicare Part D prescription drug plans using a transparent, evidence-based process.

The goal is to encourage:

- Verification over assumptions
- Primary sources over marketing materials
- Explicit documentation of uncertainties
- Comparison of total annual cost rather than premium alone
- Consideration of future medication needs

SecretPlan is not an insurance product, enrollment service, recommendation engine, or financial advisor.

All results should be treated as decision-support information.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Verify Before Recommending

Recommendations should be based on:

- Official Medicare information
- Official plan documents
- Current plan-year formularies
- Pharmacy network directories
- Verified pricing tools

If information cannot be verified, the uncertainty should be disclosed.

The methodology favors incomplete truth over fabricated certainty.

---

## 2. Optimize Total Annual Cost

Many beneficiaries focus on monthly premium.

This can be misleading.

A low-premium plan may result in significantly higher annual spending because of:

- Deductibles
- Tier placement
- Coinsurance
- Specialty drug pricing
- Pharmacy network differences

Therefore, SecretPlan evaluates:

```text
Annual Premium
+
Annual Drug Costs
=
Total Annual Cost
```

whenever reliable information is available.

---

## 3. Evaluate Actual Medications

Medicare Part D plans can produce dramatically different costs for the same individual.

A plan cannot be evaluated accurately without:

- Exact medication names
- Strengths
- Quantities
- Fill frequency

SecretPlan therefore centers the analysis around the applicant's actual medication list.

---

## 4. Verify Pharmacy-Specific Pricing

Drug costs often vary between pharmacies.

SecretPlan does not assume that:

- All pharmacies are preferred
- All network pharmacies have identical pricing
- Chain-wide pricing is identical

Whenever possible, analyses should consider:

- Preferred pharmacies
- Standard pharmacies
- Mail-order pharmacies
- Independent pharmacies

using location-specific information.

---

## 5. Prioritize Medication Access

Cost is important but should not be the only factor.

SecretPlan evaluates:

- Formulary inclusion
- Coverage restrictions
- Prior authorization requirements
- Step therapy requirements
- Quantity limits
- Specialty-pharmacy requirements

A lower-cost plan may not be the best choice if medication access is substantially worse.

---

# Future Medication Flexibility

## Why It Matters

Many Medicare beneficiaries begin a year with one medication profile and end with another.

A plan that performs well for current medications may perform poorly for medications prescribed later.

Therefore SecretPlan encourages evaluation of:

- Formulary breadth
- Coverage depth
- Restriction frequency
- Specialty-drug coverage
- Pharmacy network strength

This assessment is inherently less precise than evaluating current medications.

Future coverage analyses should be presented as qualitative assessments rather than guarantees.

---

# Source Priority

When conflicting information exists, use sources in the following order.

## Tier 1 Sources

Preferred sources:

- Medicare.gov
- Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)
- Official plan Evidence of Coverage (EOC)
- Official plan formulary documents
- Official plan pharmacy directories

These sources should take precedence whenever discrepancies occur.

---

## Tier 2 Sources

Secondary sources:

- SHIP resources
- State insurance publications
- Plan summary documents
- Official insurer comparison tools

Useful, but generally subordinate to Tier 1 sources.

---

## Tier 3 Sources

Reference sources:

- GoodRx
- Cost Plus Drugs
- SingleCare
- Optum Perks
- Consumer information websites

These may provide useful cash-price comparisons but should not be treated as authoritative plan information.

---

# Cost Estimation Method

Whenever possible:

```text
Estimated Annual Cost =
Annual Premiums
+
Expected Deductible Payments
+
Expected Copays
+
Expected Coinsurance
```

Analyses should clearly distinguish:

- Verified values
- Estimated values
- Assumptions

Premiums should not be confused with out-of-pocket drug spending.

---

# Cash Price Comparisons

SecretPlan encourages comparison against cash-pay alternatives when appropriate.

Potential examples include:

- GoodRx
- Cost Plus Drugs
- SingleCare
- Optum Perks
- Pharmacy-specific cash pricing

However:

- Cash prices change frequently
- Prices are not guaranteed
- Cash purchases may not count toward Medicare Part D spending thresholds

Comparisons should explain these limitations.

---

# Ranking Methodology

Plans should generally be ranked using the following sequence.

## Primary Factors

1. Medication coverage
2. Estimated annual cost
3. Pharmacy compatibility

## Secondary Factors

4. Formulary breadth
5. Utilization restrictions
6. Mail-order options
7. CMS Star Rating

The exact weighting may vary depending on the applicant's stated priorities.

---

# Confidence Ratings

SecretPlan recommends assigning confidence levels.

## High Confidence

Most data points verified from current sources.

Minimal assumptions required.

---

## Medium Confidence

Most major data verified.

Some pricing or pharmacy assumptions required.

---

## Low Confidence

Significant information missing.

Major assumptions required.

Recommendation may change materially if missing data becomes available.

---

# Limitations

SecretPlan does not:

- Guarantee plan availability
- Guarantee pricing
- Guarantee formulary status
- Provide legal advice
- Provide tax advice
- Provide medical advice
- Enroll beneficiaries in Medicare
